# MITRE CWE: copyright and terms of use

TriageBot quotes text from the MITRE CWE catalogue (the `Description`,
`Extended_Description` and `Potential_Mitigations` of a weakness) in the
guidance it shows the AI, and the semantic retrieval mode builds its search
query from a weakness's name, alternate terms and description. MITRE's terms
allow this on condition that every copy reproduces MITRE's copyright
designation and the licence, which are copied below.

- Source: <https://cwe.mitre.org/about/termsofuse.html> (page last updated
  July 20, 2023), fetched on 8 Nov 2026.
- The catalogue itself is not committed; it is fetched once into
  `Context/tmp/cwe/` by `python -m triagebot.nodes.context.cwe_catalogue`.

The text below is copied verbatim from that page.
